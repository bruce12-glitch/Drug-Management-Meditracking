## Customer

1. Open the site → **Login** → account type **Customer**.
2. **HOME** — profile card.
3. **BUY** — enter a quantity and order. Stock is checked server-side.
4. **ORDERS** — your purchases with product name, qty, seller, time.
5. **MEDITRACKER**
   - **Today's Medicine** — Yes / No / Remove / Reset Status
   - **Add Medicine** — name, day cycle, food, reminder, dates
   - **History** — taken / not taken log
   - **Settings** — theme, accent, English/Tamil, reminders

## Vendor

1. Login → account type **Seller**.
2. **HOME** — pharmacy profile.
3. **ADD** — new product (id, dates, price, opening stock).
4. **RESTOCK** — add quantity to an existing item.
5. **ORDERS** — customer purchases of your stock.

Vendors cannot open MediTracker. Customers cannot open vendor pages.

## Register

- **Register As Customer** — creates a shopper + empty tracker settings.
- **Register As Vendor** — creates a seller with an empty catalogue.
